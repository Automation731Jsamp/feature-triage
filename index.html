    How ranking works

    Each request gets a score: reuse × 2, minus effort, minus risk. Reuse is 1 for one firm, 2 for some and 3 for most. Effort is 1 for small, 2 for medium and 3 for large. Risk is 1 for low, 2 for medium and 3 for high. Higher scores rank first, and ties go to the request with the higher confidence. High-risk requests are gated: they rank after everything else, whatever their score, because supervision design has to be approved before anyone builds them. Click a column header to re-sort; the Rank column always shows the default order. Reuse, effort and risk are my judgments on made-up data, not measurements.

      About this prototype
      • I wrote the sorted results by hand. No AI produced them, and nothing here has been measured.
      • The three firms are made up and small. Real acquired firms differ in size, custodian and platform.
      • Reuse, effort and risk are my judgments. The compliance touchpoints are prompts for review, not legal conclusions.
      • A real version would need your data, your supervision model and a compliance reviewer.
